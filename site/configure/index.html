Set up your button

Plug the button into this computer or phone with USB, then connect.

  • Your browser will ask to use MIDI devices. Allow it.
  • Works in Chrome and Edge (desktop and Android). Safari and iOS can’t do this.
  • Close music apps that hold MIDI ports open if the button isn’t found.
  • New board? Load the firmware first.
